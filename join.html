Offer Your Help
For individuals

Join the early community

No account to create, no password to remember. Tell us roughly who you are and we'll tell you when something relevant opens up.

We'll only email you about One Jewish Community — no sharing, no selling, unsubscribe any time. See the privacy notice.

For organizations

Get your organization listed

Listing is free and stays free. We describe what you do and link to your official website — we don't take registrations, run your programs, or speak for you.

✅

What you get

A clear profile in the Partner Hub, a direct link to your site, and placement in the search and filters people actually use to find programs like yours.

🖋️

You control the words

You review the description before it goes up, and you can ask for a change or removal at any time. Your logo is only used with written permission.

💸

What it costs

Nothing. There is no paid placement, no sponsored ranking and no advertising. Listing order follows category, not who pays.

We'll reply before publishing anything about your organization.
