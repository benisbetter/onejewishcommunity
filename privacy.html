Last updated 18 September 2026

What we collect

If you just read the site: nothing. There is no analytics, no tracking pixel, no advertising network and no cookie banner, because there are no cookies to consent to. The site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

If you submit a form — the sign-up form, the organization listing form, the contact form or the suggestion form — we receive what you typed into it. That is typically your name, your email address, and your message. We ask for a city or campus only to know where community interest is; it is optional and free text, not a precise location.

Fonts are served from this site itself, so loading a page does not tell any third-party font service that you were here.

What we do with it

  • Replying to you. If you wrote to us, we use your email to write back.
  • Telling you when something opens. If you joined the list, we email you when community groups or features launch, plus an occasional short note about opportunities. You can stop this at any time by replying and saying so.
  • Fixing the site. Corrections and suggestions get used to update listings.

We do not sell your information, rent it, trade it, or hand it to partner organizations. If you tell us you're interested in an organization, we do not pass your details to them — you contact them yourself through their own website.

Who else is involved

Being honest about the plumbing, because "we don't share your data" is rarely the whole story:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site. Like any web host, GitHub's servers process the requests your browser makes, which includes your IP address. That is standard web infrastructure, and it is covered by GitHub's own privacy statement.
  • A form processor receives form submissions and forwards them to our inbox. It sees what you typed into the form.
  • Links out. Every partner, event and opportunity link takes you to an organization's own website. Once you click through, you're on their site under their rules — we have no visibility into what happens there, and their privacy policy applies, not ours.

If you are under 18

This site is meant to be usable by teens. You can browse everything without giving any information at all. Please don't put your home address, phone number, school schedule or anything else identifying into a form — we don't need it and we don't want it. If you're under 13, ask a parent or guardian before signing up for anything. If a parent or guardian wants information about their child removed, write to us and it will be deleted, no questions asked.

Your choices

  • See it. Ask what we hold about you and we'll tell you.
  • Delete it. Ask us to delete it and we will, including from the mailing list.
  • Correct it. Tell us what's wrong and we'll change it.

Use the contact form for any of these. There is no process to go through and no form to fill in twice.

How long we keep things

Contact messages are kept while they're useful and deleted when they're not. Mailing list entries are kept until you unsubscribe. If the project shuts down, the list gets deleted rather than handed to anyone else.

Terms of use

What this site is. One Jewish Community is an independent directory. It describes organizations, events and opportunities, and links to their official websites. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or speaking for any organization listed here unless that page says otherwise.

Accuracy. We try hard to keep listings current, and we date them so you can see how fresh they are. But programs change, deadlines move and pages disappear. Always confirm details on the organization's own website before relying on them — particularly eligibility, cost and deadlines. If you find something out of date, tell us.

No responsibility for third parties. We don't run the programs listed here, don't vet them beyond checking they're real and reachable, and can't be responsible for your experience with them.

Organizations. If you represent an organization listed here and want the description changed or the listing removed, write to us and it will be done. Logos are used only with written permission.

Content you send us. If you submit a story or suggestion, you're giving us permission to publish it on this site, with the name you provide or anonymously if you ask. You keep ownership of it, and you can ask us to take it down.

Changes to this page

If this changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes and anyone on the mailing list gets told. Small wording fixes just happen.
